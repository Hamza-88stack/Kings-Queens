# Kings & Queens Local SEO Strategy

Prepared: 26 July 2026

## Goal

Make Kings & Queens the strongest organic and Google Maps result for high-intent
dry cleaning, laundry, alterations, and collection/delivery searches around
Waterloo and the surrounding SE1 catchment.

Primary commercial objective:

- Increase calls, WhatsApp conversations, collection bookings, and store visits.
- Win the Waterloo/SE1 local pack before expanding to nearby collection areas.
- Build authority on the established `knqdcl.co.uk` domain.

## Strategic Principle

Google says local visibility is mainly determined by relevance, distance, and
prominence. This plan maps directly to those factors:

- Relevance: dedicated service pages, accurate GBP services, and local content.
- Distance: Waterloo-first targeting and honest service-area coverage.
- Prominence: reviews, citations, local links, partnerships, and brand mentions.

Official reference:
https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091

## Page Content Standard

Every core service page should contain:

- One clear H1 combining the service and location naturally
- A direct answer in the first paragraph: service, area, turnaround, and CTA
- Real process and garment/fabric details
- Starting prices or an honest explanation of how quotes work
- Collection/delivery coverage and restrictions
- Relevant before/after or in-store photographs
- Two or three real reviews specific to that service
- Visible address, phone, WhatsApp, opening hours, and map/directions
- Four to six useful FAQs based on customer questions
- Links to pricing, booking, related services, and the Waterloo homepage

Area pages should additionally include:

- Real travel/collection details for that area
- Relevant stations, streets, hotels, offices, or landmarks
- Actual postcode coverage
- A local customer example or operational proof
- Unique copy and imagery, not a location-name substitution template

Suggested length is not a ranking factor. Aim for complete answers, generally
700-1,200 useful words for a main service page and 500-900 for an area page.

## Google Business Profile Plan

### Profile setup

- Confirm the profile is verified and owned by the business.
- Use the real-world business name only; do not add keywords to the name.
- Select the most specific eligible primary category, expected to be
  `Dry cleaner`.
- Add only accurate secondary categories such as laundry service, launderette,
  clothing alteration service, and shoe repair where those categories are
  available and the services are genuinely provided.
- Complete every service with a short original description and a starting price
  where possible.
- Use the custom-domain homepage as the website URL.
- Use the collection booking page as the appointment URL.
- Add UTM tracking to GBP links:
  `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`
- Keep regular and holiday hours current.
- Add all supported attributes, payment methods, accessibility details, and
  service options accurately.

### Photos and updates

Publish real images, not only generated or stock-style imagery:

- Storefront in daylight and at night
- Interior and service counter
- Team members
- Cleaning, pressing, tailoring, and packaging process
- Before/after results with customer permission
- Collection vehicle and branded garment covers
- Specialist garments and hotel/commercial work where permitted

Add at least four strong new photos each month. Publish useful GBP updates for
seasonal services, turnaround changes, wedding season, duvets, coats, and
collection slots.

### Reviews

- Ask every satisfied customer after successful collection or delivery.
- Use the direct Google review link in WhatsApp and on a counter QR card.
- Do not offer rewards and do not filter customers before showing the review
  option.
- Ask for an honest review, not a scripted keyword review.
- Reply to every review within two working days.
- Mention the service and resolution naturally in responses where relevant.
- Track review velocity, rating, and recurring complaints monthly.

Google's review guidance:
https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122

## Technical SEO Requirements

The current Vite app changes metadata in the browser. Search engines can render
JavaScript, but route-level titles, schema, and social previews are more reliable
when each route returns complete HTML from the server or build output.

Required technical work:

- Pre-render or server-render every indexable route.
- Return a unique title, description, canonical, Open Graph tags, and JSON-LD in
  the initial HTML for each page.
- Use `LocalBusiness`/`DryCleaningOrLaundry` schema for the business entity.
- Use `Service` schema on service pages and `BreadcrumbList` on inner pages.
- Keep one stable business `@id` across all schema.
- Add `sameAs` links to verified social and business profiles.
- Use accurate latitude/longitude, phone, address, URL, and opening hours.
- Do not mark up reviews copied from third-party platforms as self-hosted review
  schema unless they meet Google's rules.
- Add descriptive image filenames, dimensions, alt text, WebP/AVIF variants, and
  lazy loading below the fold.
- Keep Core Web Vitals healthy on mobile.
- Add proper 404 handling and redirect retired URLs one-to-one.
- Keep the XML sitemap limited to canonical, indexable pages.
- Add Search Console and GA4, and verify conversion events.
