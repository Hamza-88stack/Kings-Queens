# Local SEO Website Hierarchy

Prepared: 26 July 2026

This document defines the landing-page structure, keyword ownership, URL rules,
content requirements, and publishing order for Kings & Queens.

It supplements `LOCAL_SEO_STRATEGY.md`.

## Core Decision

The physical shop is in Waterloo at:

Kings & Queens Dry Cleaning & Laundrette  
221 Waterloo Road  
London SE1 8XH

Therefore:

- The homepage owns the main `Waterloo + SE1` search intent.
- The full postcode `SE1 8XH` appears in the address, schema, directions, and
  local copy on the homepage.
- We do not create a second `/waterloo` or `/se1-8xh` page that competes with the
  homepage.
- Nearby pages combine a real neighbourhood with its postcode district where
  practical, for example `Elephant and Castle + SE17`.
- We do not create one page for every individual delivery postcode.

An exact full postcode normally has very little non-brand commercial search
volume. The broader district and neighbourhood terms are the primary keywords.

Recommended homepage title:

`Dry Cleaners & Laundrette in Waterloo, SE1 | Kings & Queens`

Recommended homepage H1:

`Premium Dry Cleaning and Laundry in Waterloo`

Primary homepage keywords:

- dry cleaners Waterloo
- dry cleaning Waterloo
- laundrette Waterloo
- laundry service Waterloo
- dry cleaners SE1
- laundry service SE1
- dry cleaners near Waterloo Station

Secondary homepage phrases:

- best dry cleaners in Waterloo
- top-rated laundrette in SE1
- dry cleaners near SE1 8XH
- laundry collection and delivery Waterloo

Use `best` or `top-rated` only when the claim is supported by a current,
verifiable rating or award. Reviews and proof are safer than an unsupported
superlative.

## Landing Page Template

Every location page should follow this structure:

1. Breadcrumbs
2. H1 with service category and location
3. Immediate local value proposition and collection CTA
4. Trust strip with only verified facts
5. Local service overview
6. Collection and delivery details for that area
7. Popular services and starting prices
8. Local proof, review, or approved case study
9. Location-specific landmarks, routes, and postcode coverage
10. Four to six local FAQs
11. Related service and nearby-area links
12. Address, phone, WhatsApp, hours, map, and final CTA

Recommended first paragraph pattern:

`Kings & Queens provides professional dry cleaning, laundry and alterations for
homes, hotels and businesses in [AREA]. Collections are handled from our
Waterloo shop at 221 Waterloo Road, London SE1 8XH, with [REAL TURNAROUND] and
[REAL DELIVERY TERMS].`

## Unique-Content Publishing Gate

A location page should remain unpublished or `noindex` until it contains at
least five of these unique local signals:

- Genuine collection schedule for the area
- Real postcode coverage
- Local landmark or station directions
- Area-specific service demand
- Real customer review from the area
- Approved client or hotel example
- Unique local photograph
- Area-specific price, minimum, or delivery information
- Named streets/buildings genuinely served
- Useful local FAQ not copied from another page

Changing only the location name is not sufficient.

Google defines doorway abuse as creating substantially similar pages for
specific location queries that funnel users to the same destination:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies

## Postcode Rules

### Recommended

- Use postcode districts such as `SE1`, `SE17`, `SE11`, and `SW1`.
- Combine a district with the best-known neighbourhood in the URL and title.
- Show all genuinely covered full postcodes inside the page content.
- Use the exact physical postcode `SE1 8XH` prominently on the homepage.
- Mention nearby postcodes in delivery tables only when operationally accurate.

### Not recommended

- One landing page for every full postcode.
- Pages such as `/dry-cleaners-se1-8xh`, `/dry-cleaners-se1-8ty`, and dozens of
  near-identical variants.
- Creating both `/dry-cleaners-se1` and `/dry-cleaners-waterloo` when they target
  the same intent.
- Listing areas outside the real delivery radius.
- Auto-generated copy that swaps only the area name.
- Using `best` in every title without verifiable support.

Keep titles natural. Do not force every variation into the title or H1.

## Schema Pattern

Homepage:

- `LocalBusiness`
- `DryCleaningOrLaundry`
- Accurate physical address, geo, phone, hours, image, and URL

Service pages:

- `Service`
- `BreadcrumbList`
- Link provider to the stable business `@id`

Area pages:

- `Service`
- `areaServed` for the specific neighbourhood/postcode district
- `BreadcrumbList`

Do not create fake business locations or separate `LocalBusiness` entities for
areas where there is no physical branch.

## Success Criteria

The architecture is working when:

- Google indexes only the custom-domain canonical versions.
- The homepage ranks for Waterloo/SE1 terms without competing with another local
  page.
- Each service page receives impressions for its own service cluster.
- Each area page receives impressions for its own neighbourhood/postcode terms.
- Organic calls, WhatsApp starts, direction requests, and collection bookings
  increase.
- No group of pages has nearly identical content or zero useful local detail.
